About the prototype

How to read the data.

Live market pricing, observed movement, and sample patterns are different kinds of information.

01

Follow live markets

Choose a sport to see available scores and current odds. Coverage depends on provider availability.

02

Read implied probability

Odds can be converted to an implied probability, but this includes the bookmaker margin and is not the true chance of an outcome.

03

Watch observed movement

Changes are shown only between refreshes. Neither recent market movement nor the sample patterns predict what happens next.
