Methodology and limitations

What the projection does and does not mean.

A simple, inspectable baseline that compares recent returns. It cannot reliably predict future prices.

01

Market data

Daily historical prices are retrieved from Yahoo Finance's chart endpoint. Data may be delayed or temporarily unavailable if the provider rate-limits its unofficial endpoint; this is not a real-time trading feed.

02

Projection

The estimate blends the selected stock's mean daily return over the latest 60 aligned sessions with the mean return of its configured comparison securities. It compounds that blended mean over the next two sessions.

03

Confidence and range

The confidence percentage is the model's directional accuracy over up to 60 rolling, out-of-sample two-session historical tests, not the probability that a forecast will be correct. The 80% range assumes historical return dispersion continues; it is not guaranteed to contain the future price.

Important

Historical performance does not predict future results. The model omits earnings, news, macroeconomic changes, trading costs, and other factors. If too little comparable history is available, the app reports that the confidence estimate cannot be calculated.
